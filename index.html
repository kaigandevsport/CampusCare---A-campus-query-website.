A LITTLE MORE CONTEXT

Help the right people
understand the issue.

Reports are easier to act on when they are clear, specific, and safe to share.

01

Be precise about place

Add the building name and, if you know it, the floor, room, entrance, or closest landmark.

02

Stick to what you saw

Include when you noticed it and what is affected. Avoid guessing about causes or naming other people.

03

Keep yourself safe

Don’t approach hazards to take a photo. For immediate danger, contact campus security or emergency services.
